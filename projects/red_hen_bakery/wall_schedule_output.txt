NOTES:
  1. Walls retaining <= 4 ft unbalanced backfill: per IRC prescriptive requirements.
  2. All dimensions are nominal. Stem thickness = constant 8" for walls < 10 ft.
  3. For walls >= 10 ft, two options are shown: 8" and 10" stem/footing.
  4. Heel length is auto-sized to satisfy stability (FS_OT >= 1.5, FS_SL >= 1.5, q <= q_allow).
  5. Stem reinforcing is vertical steel on the earth-side face (tension side).
  6. Heel reinforcing is top steel; toe reinforcing is bottom steel.
  7. T&S steel (transverse): 0.0018 x Ag = 0.0018 x 12 x t  (provide in horizontal direction).
  8. Cover = 2" (cast against soil) per ACI 318-19 Table 20.6.1.3.1.
  9. Verify all soil parameters with project geotechnical report before final design.
 10. Embedment = 1'-0" assumed. Adjust based on frost depth and site conditions.
