## Usage Notes

- Fill in all fields before beginning calculations
- Revision log updated with every issuance
- Checker and Approver sign/date only after review is complete
- Mark "PRELIMINARY" or "FOR REVIEW" on cover until final
- Final version receives PE stamp adjacent to Approved By line
