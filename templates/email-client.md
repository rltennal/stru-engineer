# Template: Client Email Templates

## General Guidelines

- Professional but personable tone
- Clear subject lines with project reference
- Action items and deadlines explicitly stated
- Respond within 24 hours (even if just to acknowledge)
- Every email should answer: What do you need to know? What do I need from you? By when?

## Usage Notes

- Always personalize — never send a template verbatim without customization
- Include project name in every subject line
- Attach documents referenced in the email (do not make client ask)
- If bad news (delay, additional cost, problem), communicate early and with a proposed solution
- Follow up on items sent if no response within 5 business days
