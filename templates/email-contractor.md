# Template: Contractor Email Templates

## General Guidelines

- Professional, direct, and unambiguous
- Reference specific drawing sheets, detail numbers, and specification sections
- Clear directives with reasons
- Every RFI response includes: the question restated, the answer, and the drawing/spec reference
- Document everything — these emails may become project records

---

**Subject:** [Project Name] — RFI #[Number] Response: [Brief Description]

[Clear, direct answer. Reference specific drawing sheets, details,
and code sections.]

[If applicable: revised detail or sketch attached / see attached SK-#]

- Sheet: [S-XX, Detail X/SX.X]

the contract documents. [If it does: describe the change and confirm
whether a formal change order / ASI is required.]

Ray Tennal, PE
RaBuilt Engineering

## Usage Notes

- RFI responses are project record documents — write them carefully
- Always restate the question to avoid misunderstanding
- Be direct — contractors need clear answers, not hedging
- Reference specific drawings and details — "see the plans" is not an acceptable response
- When a field fix is needed, provide a sketch (even a simple one) rather than only words
- cc the architect and owner on items that affect design intent or cost
- Track all RFIs in a log with dates received and responded
