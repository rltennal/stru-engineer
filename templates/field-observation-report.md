## Usage Notes

- Complete the same day as the visit (while observations are fresh)
- Photographs: take many, organize by observation item number
- Be specific about locations — use grid lines, elevations, compass directions
- Non-conformance items must include required corrective action
- Track open items from previous visits — do not let them drop
- The conformance statement is a professional opinion — word it carefully
- Distribute within 24 hours of visit
