## Usage Notes

- Invoice numbering: INV-YYYY-001, INV-YYYY-002, etc. (sequential per calendar year)
- Invoice the same day each month (consistency aids client's AP process)
- Hourly descriptions should be meaningful: "structural design — floor framing" not just "engineering"
- Lump sum: invoice at meaningful milestones, not arbitrary percentages
- Always include the contract summary — helps client track the project budget
- Attach backup for reimbursable expenses (receipts, mileage log)
- Send invoices as PDF
- Track invoice status: Sent → Received → Paid → Record payment date and check/reference number
- Follow up on overdue invoices: 15 days (gentle reminder), 30 days (formal), 45 days (demand), 60 days (consider next steps)
