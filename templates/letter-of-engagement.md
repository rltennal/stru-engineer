       - Payment due within [30] days of invoice date.
       - Interest of [1.5]% per month on unpaid balances beyond [30] days.
       - [Optional: Retainer of $[Amount] due upon execution of this

   [Describe anticipated timeline or state "to be coordinated with

   - Executed engagement letter

   Services will be performed in accordance with the standard of care

   Any disputes shall first be submitted to mediation. If unresolved,

   Either party may terminate this agreement with [14] days written notice.

## Usage Notes

- Customize scope of services for each project — do not use generic language
- Fee structure should match the complexity and risk of the project
- Limitation of liability clause: consult with attorney for appropriate language
- Always execute engagement letter BEFORE starting work
- Keep signed copies in project file
- Reference this engagement letter in all invoices
