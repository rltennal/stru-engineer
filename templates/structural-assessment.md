## Usage Notes

- Customize sections for each project — delete sections that don't apply, add project-specific sections as needed
- Executive Summary is the most important section — write it last after completing the full report
- Be specific in observations and findings — vague reports have no value
- Clearly separate what you observed (fact) from what you believe (opinion)
- Include a photo appendix — always
- If analysis is not in scope, do not include Section 6 (or state "Analysis was not within the scope of this assessment")
- Review for completeness and consistency before stamping
